## 1. Content Review Workflow

### Step 1: First Pass — Deep Listen / Watch
- Watch the full episode at 1.5x–2x speed.
- Keep a running timer/notepad for timestamps you flag.
- Note the **overall theme** and **target audience** of the episode.

### Step 2: Second Pass — Flag & Categorise
- Revisit flagged moments and categorise each into one of the four buckets (see §2).
- Rank each moment: **🔥 Must-Clip**, **👍 Good**, **🤷 Optional**.

### Step 3: Write Clip Briefs
- For every 🔥 and 👍 moment, fill out a Clip Brief (see §4).
- Assign each clip to the best-fit platform (see §3).

### Step 4: Hand-off to Video Editor
- Place completed briefs in `/home/team/shared/clip_briefs/`.
- Notify the Video Editor with timestamps, clip type, and platform.

## 2. Clip-Worthy Moment Criteria

### 2.1 Quotable Lines

**What to look for:**
- A self-contained sentence or phrase that lands hard on its own (no context needed).
- Clever metaphors, analogies, or one-liners.
- Lines the audience will screenshot and share.
- "I wish I'd said that" observations.

**Examples:**
- "Perfectionism is just fear in a fancy suit."
- "The biggest moat in business is speed of execution."
- "Most people overestimate what they can do in a day and underestimate what they can do in a year."

**How to identify while watching:**
- Did the host/guest pause after saying it? That's a sign they know it's punchy.
- Did the co-host or audience react (laugh, lean in, repeat it)?
- Could you caption it as a pull-quote without editing?

### 2.2 Hot Takes

**What to look for:**
- A contrarian opinion or bold prediction.
- Statements that challenge conventional wisdom.
- "Unpopular opinion" framing.
- Spicy takes that will spark debate in comments.

**Examples:**
- "Hiring for culture fit is overrated — hire for culture add instead."
- "SEO is dead. AI search killed it."
- "The 4-day work week is a trap for junior employees."

**How to identify while watching:**
- Does the speaker say "most people think X, but actually…"?
- Does the energy shift — voice gets louder, faster, more emphatic?
- Will this make someone want to reply with their own take?

### 2.3 Educational Insights

**What to look for:**
- A step-by-step explanation of how something works.
- Frameworks, formulas, or mental models.
- Actionable advice the audience can implement immediately.
- "Here's exactly how I did X" moments.

**Examples:**
- "Here's the 3-step framework I use to validate any business idea in 48 hours."
- "The reason your cold emails get ignored is you're leading with your product, not their problem."
- "Let me walk you through how we built our waitlist to 10K in 2 weeks."

**How to identify while watching:**
- Does the speaker draw a diagram, hold up a book, or use visual aids?
- Does the speaker say "here's the playbook" or "let me break this down"?
- Could this stand alone as a quick tutorial?

### 2.4 Storytelling Beats

**What to look for:**
- Personal anecdotes with emotional arcs (setup → conflict → resolution).
- "I almost quit" / "I lost everything" / "And then it clicked" moments.
- Origin stories. Failure stories. Underdog stories.
- Vivid, sensory details that paint a picture.

**Examples:**
- "Two years ago I was sleeping on my friend's couch with $47 in my account…"
- "I remember the exact moment I knew the business was going to work…"
- "Here's the mistake that cost us $100K — don't make it yourself."

**How to identify while watching:**
- Does the pace slow down and the speaker get more reflective?
- Are there specific names, dates, locations mentioned?
- Does the story have a clear beginning, middle, and end?

### Rating System

| Rating | Meaning | Action |
|--------|---------|--------|
| 🔥 **Must-Clip** | Strong emotional reaction, highly shareable, high debate potential | Brief required. Fast-track to editor. |
| 👍 **Good** | Solid moment, fits one platform well | Brief required. Add to queue. |
| 🤷 **Optional** | Interesting but not urgent | Brief if time allows. Archive otherwise. |

## 3. Platform-Specific Requirements

### 3.1 YouTube Shorts

| Spec | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| **Aspect ratio** | 9:16 (vertical, 1080×1920) |
| **Max duration** | 60 seconds (sweet spot: 15–45s) |
| **Caption style** | Large, bold, centre-aligned. 2–3 words per line max. |
| **Hook** | First 1–2 seconds must grab attention. Start with the punchline. |
| **Pacing** | Fast cuts. No dead air. Minimal filler words. |
| **Best content types** | Hot takes, quotable lines, storytelling beats with high emotional impact, educational quick tips |
| **CTAs** | "Follow for more", "Comment your take", "Save this" |
| **Thumbnail** | Single text overlay with the core quote. High contrast. |
| **Captions** | Auto-captions (burned-in) are essential. Use yellow/white words with black outline. |

**Narrative Structure:**
```
0:00–0:02  Hook (the punchline, bold statement, or question)
0:02–0:15  Core insight / the "why"
0:15–0:45  Expansion or example
0:45–0:60  Recap + CTA
```

### 3.2 Instagram Reels

| Spec | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| **Aspect ratio** | 9:16 (vertical, 1080×1920) |
| **Max duration** | 90 seconds (sweet spot: 7–30s) |
| **Caption style** | Stylish, trendy fonts. Text overlays that animate in. |
| **Hook** | Visual + text hook in first second. Use trending audio when possible. |
| **Pacing** | Snappy. Every 3–5 seconds a new visual or text element. |
| **Best content types** | Hot takes, quick educational insights, storytelling beats |
| **Aesthetic** | Polished but not corporate. Trendy transitions. |
| **Captions** | Burned-in captions in a clean sans-serif font. Speaker name on first appearance. |
| **Trends** | Leverage trending audio, transitions, and text styles. |

**Narrative Structure:**
```
0:00–0:01  Visual/text hook
0:01–0:10  Core message
0:10–0:20  Elaboration (text callouts)
0:20–0:30  Punchline / Call to action
```

### 3.3 LinkedIn Posts

| Spec | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| **Format** | Text + image/video + caption |
| **Text length** | 900–1,200 characters (sweet spot for engagement) |
| **Hook** | First 3 lines must stop the scroll. Lead with the contrarian take or story hook. |
| **Post structure** | Hook → Story/Insight → Lesson → CTA |
| **Best content types** | Educational insights, storytelling beats, thought leadership hot takes |
| **Visual** | 1–2 minute vertical clip from the episode OR a text-based carousel (PDF/images) |
| **Hashtags** | 3–5 relevant hashtags at the end (not in body) |
| **Tone** | Professional but conversational. First-person voice. |

**Post Structure Template:**
```
[THE HOOK — 1 sentence that stops the scroll]

[THE STORY / INSIGHT — 3–5 short paragraphs]
- Open with context
- Drop the insight
- Add a real example

[THE LESSON — 1–2 punchy sentences]

[THE CTA — Question for comments or link]
- What's one thing you'd add?

#hashtag1 #hashtag2 #hashtag3
```

### 3.4 X (Twitter) Threads

| Spec | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| **Format** | Multi-post thread |
| **Length** | 3–10 tweets per thread |
| **Hook tweet** | First tweet must be fully self-contained (gets the idea across even if no one clicks "show more") |
| **Best content types** | Hot takes, educational frameworks, step-by-step insights |
| **Visual** | 1–2 images or short clips embedded in the thread |
| **Tone** | Punchy, direct, slightly informal. Short paragraphs. |
| **Spacing** | Blank line between each tweet in the thread for readability |

**Thread Structure Template:**
```
Tweet 1: THE HOOK — Controversial take or provocative question
         "Most people think X. But here's the truth: Y."

Tweet 2: WHY — Context for the take
         "Here's why this matters right now..."

Tweet 3: THE INSIGHT — Core teaching
         "The framework is simple: 1) ..., 2) ..., 3) ..."

Tweet 4: EXAMPLE — Real-world proof
         "Here's how I applied this..."

Tweet 5: THE URGE — Call to action
         "If you're doing X, try Y instead. Your audience will thank you."

Tweet 6: REPOST REQUEST (optional)
         "RT if this was helpful. Follow @handle for more."
```

## 5. Tone & Branding Guidelines

### 5.1 Voice Principles

| Principle | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Confident, not arrogant** | We speak from experience. We don't claim to have all the answers. |
| **Clear, not dumbed-down** | No jargon unless the audience demands it. Plain language wins. |
| **Conversational, not casual** | Like a smart friend explaining something at a coffee shop. |
| **Opinionated, not offensive** | Hot takes have a home. They should invite discussion, not outrage. |

### 5.2 Platform Tone Adjustments

| Platform | Tone |
|----------|------|
| **YouTube Shorts** | Energetic, punchy, slightly informal. Direct eye contact. Fast pace. |
| **Instagram Reels** | Friendly, trend-aware. Visual-first. Personality-forward. |
| **LinkedIn** | Professional but personal. "I learned this the hard way" energy. |
| **X (Twitter)** | Bold, concise, spicy. Fewer words = more impact. |

### 5.3 Brand Identity Elements

**Logo Usage:**
- Use the ClipCraft logo in the top-left corner of all short-form videos.
- Keep it small and semi-transparent (≈15% opacity or second colour).
- On LinkedIn posts, use the logo as a profile image or watermark.

**Colour Palette:**
- Primary colour for text highlights in videos: [Brand colour, e.g., #FF6B35]
- Background overlays: Dark gradient (black → transparent) for text readability
- Caption text: White with black outline = default. Brand colour for emphasis words.

**Typography:**
- Primary font: [Brand font, e.g., Inter, Montserrat, or Poppins]
- Captions: Bold weight, size 36–48px (adjust for mobile readability)
- Headlines on thumbnails: Extra bold, size 72px+

### 5.4 Accessibility
- All videos MUST have burned-in captions.
- Minimum caption size: 36px on 1080p canvas.
- High contrast: White text with thick black outline.
- Avoid fast-flashing content (>3 flashes per second).
- On LinkedIn/X, include alt text for any images/carousels.

## 6. Queue & Prioritisation

### Per-Episode Yield Targets

| Volume | Clips per Episode |
|--------|-------------------|
| **Minimum** | 3 clips (1 YT Shorts + 1 LinkedIn + 1 X Thread) |
| **Standard** | 5 clips (2 YT Shorts/Reels + 1 LinkedIn + 1 X Thread + 1 wildcard) |
| **Maximum** | 8 clips (3 short-form videos + 2 LinkedIn + 2 X Threads + 1 wildcard) |

### Priority Scoring

Score each clip on a 1–5 scale:

| Factor | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Shareability** | Will people share this without context? |
| **Debate potential** | Will it spark comments and discussion? |
| **Novelty** | Is this a fresh take or something the audience has heard before? |
| **Actionability** | Can the audience do something with this info immediately? |
| **Emotional impact** | Does it make people feel something (laughter, surprise, inspiration)? |

**Priority = Total Score**
- 20–25: 🔥 Must-Clip
- 15–19: 👍 Good
- 10–14: 🤷 Optional
- <10: Archive

## 7. Quality Checklist

Before submitting briefs to the Video Editor, verify every clip meets these standards:

### Content Review
- [ ] The clip makes sense WITHOUT the surrounding context
- [ ] There are no inside jokes or references the general audience won't get
- [ ] Timestamps are precise (to the second)
- [ ] The hook grabs attention in the first 2 seconds

### Platform Fit
- [ ] Duration fits the platform's sweet spot
- [ ] Aspect ratio is correct for the target platform
- [ ] The tone matches the platform's audience expectations

### Legal & Ethics
- [ ] The clip doesn't contain false or unverifiable claims
- [ ] The clip doesn't contain defamatory or offensive content
- [ ] No copyrighted music or content in the background
- [ ] Speaker has consented to repurposing

### Completeness
- [ ] Clip brief is fully filled out (no blank sections)
- [ ] Script outline covers hook, body, and CTA
- [ ] Platform adaptation notes are included
- [ ] Branding instructions are specified

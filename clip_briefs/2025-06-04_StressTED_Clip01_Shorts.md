## Transcript / Quote
> "I spent years telling people stress is bad for you. I believed it with my whole heart. So I wanted to change how people thought about stress. And I stand by that work to this day. But there's something I wish I'd known back then.
>
> Here's what a study of 30,000 adults in the US over 8 years found. People who experienced a lot of stress had a 43% increased risk of dying. BUT — that was only true for people who *also believed* that stress was harming their health. People who experienced a lot of stress but did NOT view stress as harmful had the *lowest* risk of death in the entire study — lower than people who had very little stress."

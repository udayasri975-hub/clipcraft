# ClipCraft — Turn 1 Video Into a Content Library

## What We Do
We turn your long-form YouTube videos, podcasts, and recordings into ready-to-post short-form content — YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, LinkedIn posts, and X threads.

**You record once. We repurpose everywhere.**

## How It Works
1. **You send us a link** — YouTube, podcast RSS, or raw file upload
2. **We identify the best moments** — quotable lines, hot takes, educational gems, storytelling beats
3. **We produce & captions** — platform-optimized clips with burned-in captions
4. **You post** — finished files delivered within 24 hours

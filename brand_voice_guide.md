## 1. Our Core Voice

ClipCraft exists in the space between **expert** and **friend**. We're the colleague who's three years ahead and willing to share the shortcuts.

| Dimension | Our Position |
|-----------|-------------|
| Formal ↔ Casual | **75% Casual** — We write like we speak |
| Corporate ↔ Personal | **80% Personal** — Stories, not stats |
| Safe ↔ Bold | **70% Bold** — Opinions welcome, nonsense called out |
| Technical ↔ Accessible | **85% Accessible** — Smart, not smart-alecky |

**The one-sentence test:** If our content wouldn't sound natural coming from a smart friend at a coffee shop, it doesn't pass.

## 2. The Four Voice Pillars

### Pillar 1: Clarity First

**What it means:**
- One idea per sentence. One takeaway per clip.
- Remove every word that doesn't do work. "In order to" → "To". "The fact that" → delete it.
- If the viewer needs to rewind to understand, we failed.

**In practice:**
- Hook = the takeaway, not a setup for a setup
- Captions = complete thoughts, not sentence fragments
- Threads = one tweet = one point

**Check yourself:** Read the copy aloud. If you stumble, rewrite.

### Pillar 2: Confident, Not Arrogant

**What it means:**
- We speak from experience and data, not ego.
- "Here's what worked for me" beats "This is the only way."
- We invite conversation, not worship.

**In practice:**
- Use "I" and "we" naturally. Own our opinions.
- Use "Here's why" framing instead of "You're doing it wrong" framing.
- Cite specific examples. Vague claims feel like hot air.

**Check yourself:** Could someone disagree without feeling attacked? If yes, it's confident. If no, soften.

### Pillar 3: Human-Scaled

**What it means:**
- We talk about audiences, not "impressions." Revenue, not "monetisation strategies."
- We use concrete numbers and real names. "I got 47 emails in 2 hours" beats "I received significant engagement."
- We share failures as generously as wins.

**In practice:**
- "3 things" not "several key components"
- "My co-founder Sarah" not "the leadership team"
- "Lost $12K on a bad hire" not "experienced talent acquisition challenges"

**Check yourself:** Would a non-expert understand this? Does it feel like a human wrote it?

### Pillar 4: Purposefully Opinionated

**What it means:**
- We take positions. Neutral content gets ignored.
- Hot takes earn engagement — but they must be grounded.
- We'd rather be interesting and right 80% of the time than boring and right 100%.

**In practice:**
- Lead posts with a take, not a question.
- Threads start with the conclusion, warm up to the proof.
- Every clip has a reason to exist: teach, provoke, inspire, or connect.

**Check yourself:** Does this content provoke a reaction? If someone reads/watches it and feels neutral, rewrite.

## 3. Platform Voice Matrix

### YouTube Shorts

| Voice element | Guideline |
|--------------|-----------|
| **Energy level** | 8/10 — High. Grab them in the first frame. |
| **Sentence length** | 5–10 words per caption line. Single idea per cut. |
| **Pronouns** | "You" first, "I" second. Make it about the viewer's takeaway. |
| **Pacing** | Fast. Every 3–5 seconds, a new visual or text element. |
| **Hook formula** | "The [X] that [Y] — and why it matters." |
| **Sign-off** | Direct CTA: "Save this for later", "Follow for more", "Comment your take" |
| **Music** | Energetic, driving. Matches the clip's emotional tone. |

**Example opening:**
> "The biggest mistake founders make when hiring? They optimise for experience instead of speed of learning."

### Instagram Reels

| Voice element | Guideline |
|--------------|-----------|
| **Energy level** | 7/10 — Approachable and trendy. Less intense than Shorts. |
| **Sentence length** | 5–8 words. Leave room for emojis and visual flair. |
| **Pronouns** | "You" heavy. Direct address. |
| **Styling** | Trend-forward. Leverage text animations and popular transitions. |
| **Hook formula** | Visual + text bomb. Let the visual do half the work. |
| **Sign-off** | "Save + share with someone who needs this" |
| **Music** | Trending audio is preferred over original. Match the vibe. |

**Example opening:**
> (Text on screen: "Stop doing this.") "If you're sending cold DMs asking for 'a quick call' — stop."

### LinkedIn Posts

| Voice element | Guideline |
|--------------|-----------|
| **Energy level** | 6/10 — Professional warmth. Write like you're emailing a respected peer. |
| **Paragraph length** | 1–3 sentences max. White space is the reader's best friend. |
| **Pronouns** | "I" for stories, "You" for lessons, "We" for shared experiences. |
| **Hook** | Must stop the scroll in the first 3 lines. Lead with the lesson, not the setup. |
| **Tone** | "I learned this the hard way" — vulnerability + expertise. |
| **Structure** | Hook → Story → Lesson → CTA |
| **Length** | 900–1,200 characters. Long enough to be meaty, short enough to read in one coffee sip. |
| **Hashtags** | 3–5 max. At the very bottom, not in the body. |

**Example opening:**
> "I shared our revenue numbers publicly once. I spent the next 72 hours defending our pricing to strangers.
>
> That's when I learned: transparency is a tool, not a strategy."

### X (Twitter) Threads

| Voice element | Guideline |
|--------------|-----------|
| **Energy level** | 9/10 — Punchy, direct, borderline spicy. |
| **Sentence length** | 1 sentence per tweet. 2 at most. |
| **Pronouns** | "Here's" and "I've found" — own the take. |
| **Hook tweet** | Must work as a standalone post. The thread is a bonus. |
| **Tone** | Bold. Contrarian when possible. "Unpopular opinion" energy. |
| **Structure** | Hook → Point 1 → Point 2 → Point 3 → CTA |
| **Spacing** | One blank line between each tweet for readability. |
| **Visuals** | 1–2 screenshots or clips embedded. Break up text walls. |

**Example hook tweet:**
> Most people think building in public is about transparency.
> It's not. It's a marketing strategy. Treat it like one.

## 4. Copy & Caption Templates

### Short-Form Video Description Template

```
[EMOJI] [The hook takeaway — 1 sentence]

Watch the full episode: [LINK]

🎥 by ClipCraft — we turn long-form content into short-form gold.
```

### LinkedIn Post Template

```
[HOOK — 1–2 lines that stop the scroll]

[STORY / CONTEXT — 2–3 short paragraphs]
- What happened
- What I learned
- Why it matters

[THE LESSON — 1 punchy sentence]
Bold or italicise for emphasis.

[CTA — Open-ended question or resource link]

#hashtag1 #hashtag2 #hashtag3
```

### X Thread Template

```
Tweet 1: [Contrarian take or bold claim]
The thread below [PREVIEW of what follows]

Tweet 2: [Why this matters — context]
Here's what most people miss about [TOPIC]:

Tweet 3: [Point 1]
[FRAMEWORK / INSIGHT] — 1 sentence
Example or data point.

Tweet 4: [Point 2]
[FRAMEWORK / INSIGHT]
Example or data point.

Tweet 5: [Point 3]
[FRAMEWORK / INSIGHT]
Example or data point.

Tweet 6: [The CTA]
Try [ACTION] instead of [OLD WAY].
Your [AUDIENCE] will thank you.

Tweet 7 (optional): RT if this was helpful.
```

### Instagram Reel Caption Template

```
[3–5 word hook in ALL CAPS or Title Case]
[2–3 lines of context]
[1 line CTA with emoji]

[3–5 relevant hashtags + #clipcraft]
```

## 5. Words We Use & Words We Avoid

### ✅ Words We Use

| Category | Words & Phrases |
|----------|----------------|
| **Action words** | Build, ship, launch, cut, double, save, flip, crack, hack, unlock |
| **Relatable words** | Honestly, real talk, truth bomb, the thing is, here's the deal |
| **Authority markers** | I've found, here's why, the framework is, the playbook, rule of thumb |
| **Engagement bait** | Save this, drop a comment, disagree? Tell me, which one are you? |
| **Conversational fillers** | Look, So here's the thing, Actually, The truth is |

### ❌ Words We Avoid

| Word/Phrase | Replace With |
|-------------|-------------|
| Leverage | Use |
| Optimise | Improve / make better |
| Synergy | Fit / connection / overlap |
| Utilise | Use |
| Paradigm shift | Big change / new way |
| Circle back | Come back to / follow up |
| Deep dive | Dig into / explore |
| Actionable insights | Things you can do / practical tips |
| Ecosystem | Space / world / community |
| Deliverables | Outputs / what you get |

**The jargon rule:** If you wouldn't say it to a friend at dinner, don't write it.

## 6. Brand Persona

If ClipCraft were a person, they'd be:

> A sharp operator who's been in the creator economy for 5+ years. They've built things, broken things, and learned the hard way. They're the person you call when you need honest feedback, not a hype man. They're generous with their playbooks but impatient with excuses. They wear sneakers to meetings and can quote both Seneca and a Rick and Morty episode in the same conversation.

**Archetype:** The Sage + The Outlaw
- **Sage:** We share knowledge freely. Our content is a shortcut for others.
- **Outlaw:** We challenge norms. Hot takes are our home turf.

**Anti-persona (who we're NOT):**
- The hype man promising overnight results
- The academic who can't explain things simply
- The guru who's always selling a course
- The cynic who tears down without building up

## 7. Visual Voice

### Typography Usage Across Copy

| Element | Style |
|---------|-------|
| **LinkedIn post body** | Regular weight. No all-caps lines (except short emphasis). |
| **LinkedIn hooks** | Bold the first line for emphasis. |
| **X tweets** | ALL CAPS for key words — sparingly. One per tweet max. |
| **Video captions** | White text, black outline. Brand colour for key words. |
| **Video CTAs** | "→ SAVE THIS" or "→ FOLLOW" on a branded background card. |

### Punctuation & Formatting

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| **Em dashes** | Use them generously — they create natural pauses. |
| **Ellipses** | For suspense or trailing thoughts… but don't overuse. |
| **Exclamation marks** | 1 per post max. More reads as shouty. |
| **Line breaks** | After every 1–3 sentences on LinkedIn. |
| **Bullet points** | Great for lists on LinkedIn and X threads. |
| **Emoji** | 1–3 per post. At the start of hooks or end of CTAs. Not in the middle of sentences. |
| **Bold** | For emphasis only — one bolded phrase per paragraph. |
| **Italics** | For internal thoughts, quotes, or irony. |

### Brand Emojis

| Context | Emojis |
|---------|--------|
| **Video descriptions** | 🎥, 🎬, 🎯, ⚡ |
| **LinkedIn posts** | 💡, 🔥, 📌, 🧠, 👇 |
| **X threads** | 🧵 (thread marker), 👀, 🔥, 💯 |
| **Lessons/learnings** | 📖, 🛠️, 💪 |
| **Hot takes** | 🗣️, ⚡, 🔥 |

## 8. Quick Reference

### Voice Cheat Sheet

| Do This | Not This |
|---------|----------|
| "Here's what I learned" | "Key learnings include" |
| "It didn't work" | "Outcomes were suboptimal" |
| "Build this in 3 steps" | "Implement a multi-phase approach" |
| "My take? [HOT TAKE]" | "Research suggests that…" |
| "You should try X" | "It is recommended to pursue X" |

### Platform Tone at a Glance

| Platform | Energy | Formality | Hook Style | Sign-off |
|----------|--------|-----------|------------|----------|
| YT Shorts | 8/10 | 2/10 | Punchline first | "Follow for more" |
| IG Reels | 7/10 | 2/10 | Visual + text bomb | "Save & share" |
| LinkedIn | 6/10 | 5/10 | Story hook | "What's your take?" |
| X (Twitter) | 9/10 | 1/10 | Contrarian claim | "RT if helpful" |

### The 5-Second Voice Check

Ask these questions before publishing any content:

1. **Would I say this to a friend?** If it sounds like a press release, rewrite.
2. **Is there a single clear takeaway?** If not, cut or reframe.
3. **Does this add something new?** We don't repeat what everyone else is saying.
4. **Would this spark a conversation?** If it's neutral, it's forgettable.
5. **Is it on-brand for ClipCraft?** Confident. Clear. Human. Opinionated.
